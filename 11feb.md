## ✅ Result
- Docker volumes were successfully created.
- Containers were tested with and without volumes.
- Data persistence was observed when volumes were attached.
- It was found that containers without volumes do not retain data.

---

## 📌 Conclusion
This experiment helped in understanding how Docker volumes work and how they help in storing data permanently.  
Volumes are essential when applications require data persistence.
